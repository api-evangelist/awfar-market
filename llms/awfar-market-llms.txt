# Awfar

> Awfar is a Cairo- and Riyadh-based digital solutions company (founded 2013) offering an integrated customer engagement and commerce platform: an omnichannel Chat Center with AI agents (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook), a cloud Call Center, and a Commerce & Delivery platform for order, inventory, and logistics management. It serves 2,000+ clients across retail, pharmacy, restaurant, e-commerce, tourism, healthcare, education, and real estate, with prebuilt integrations for Shopify, Meta, Odoo, Zid, and Salla plus custom API connections.
